A Time to Build

September 2, 2026 · Stonewall Fellowship · 1 min read

Robert E. Lee is another great man of history, and one of the finest produced from our native soil. I am writing this in the recent aftermath of the latest Stonewall Jackson statue being removed from its rightful place. It is a precarious situation for those seeking to remove the statues of men like Jackson and Lee. If these men in their conduct, speech, and fulfilment of their duties cannot be thought of as great men—then there are no great men currently walking around.

Now, I know those committed to removing the statues are not concerned with this, but are committed to the war against Heritage Americans and the continued erosion of our homeland.

People such as these have never held, even for a brief moment, the joy and dignity of building something of value, or even of doing a good deed that shall go unnoticed. All they can do is poison, destroy, and take, for the barbarians at the gate care not for the great values of the West.

Join us as we build our Fellowship of men who are builders, defenders, and stewards of the good, true, and beautiful.

They may get more statues removed, but we live in an age that beckons us to once again build—and build anew we shall.
